## 🌌 About Onco Sphere

**Onco Sphere** is a full-stack cancer institute management platform built to bring patient records, clinical reporting, researcher onboarding, administrative operations, and institutional workflows into a single digital ecosystem.

The platform provides a structured interface for healthcare and research teams to manage operational data while maintaining authentication, role-based access, reporting workflows, document uploads, and email-based communication.

> 🧠 **Goal:** Reduce fragmented workflows and provide a centralized digital environment for cancer institute operations and clinical research management.

---

## 🎯 Why Onco Sphere?

Traditional healthcare workflows often involve multiple disconnected systems for:

- 👤 Patient registration
- 📋 Medical records
- 🧪 Clinical reports
- 🔬 Researcher applications
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor information
- 🏢 Institute configuration
- 📊 Operational dashboards
- 📁 Document management
- 📧 Email notifications

**Onco Sphere brings these workflows together into one structured platform.**

# 🚀 Key Features

## 👤 Patient Management

- 📝 Register new patients
- 🔎 Search and manage patient records
- ✏️ Update patient information
- 👨‍⚕️ Manage doctor and department information
- 🧬 Store cancer type/site information
- 📈 Track cancer stage
- 💊 Track treatment status
- 📞 Maintain emergency contact information
- 🖼️ Upload cancer-related images
- 📄 Manage patient reports
- 🗂️ View complete patient profiles

---

## 🔬 Researcher Registration System

A structured multi-step researcher onboarding workflow.

### Researcher application includes:

- 👤 Personal information
- 🏛️ Institution details
- 🎓 Academic qualification
- 🧪 Research specialization
- 🔬 Research interests
- 🧬 Cancer research areas
- 📚 Previous research work
- 👨‍🏫 Supervisor information
- 🔗 Professional profiles
- 📎 Identity documents
- 📄 Institutional documents
- 📝 Letter of intent
